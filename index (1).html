Find risk early. Keep care moving. Protect every interaction.

ONCOmitra turns a community screen into a visible care journey. The prototype gives each user one clear next action, while consent, role access and human review remain built into the workflow.

1Field-ready intakeStructured symptoms, habits, photos and consent prompts work with intermittent connectivity.
2Closed-loop careRisk guidance, referral status and follow-up queues expose the next handover before a patient is lost.
3Trusted intelligenceRole-specific training and guarded AI assistance support teams without replacing clinical judgement.
Patient-centredEvery screen ends with a documented next step.
Team-readyASHA, ANM, clinician and programme views align around one journey.
Pilot-readyPrototype targets for usability, follow-up and safety can be measured from day one.

Trust by design

  • Consent and minimum-necessary data guide each intake.
  • Role access belongs to the application, not to an AI response.
  • Untrusted text, images and documents are treated as data, not instructions.
  • Clinical decisions and external actions require human review.

Training & interface guide

Role-specific onboarding for safe, consistent use of ONCOmitra. This prototype supports training; local clinical protocols and supervision remain authoritative.

Core learning pathway

Complete each module, then review the readiness check with a supervisor before accessing live patient data.

Module 01 · 12 min

Safe community screening

Prepare a respectful screening encounter and recognise when the patient needs the next level of care.

  1. Confirm identity, privacy and informed consent before entering any information.
  2. Use the guided symptom and risk-factor questions; do not diagnose from the app.
  3. Capture photos only with consent and follow the quality prompts.
  4. Escalate through the local referral protocol when red flags or uncertainty are present.
Not started
Module 02 · 10 min

Using the field interface

Practise the common workflow on a phone, tablet or shared facility computer.

  1. Use the role selector only for your own approved role.
  2. Start a record, save progress and check the offline indicator before ending the visit.
  3. Find an existing patient before creating a duplicate record.
  4. Use the patient timeline to confirm the next action and responsible facility.
Not started
Module 03 · 10 min

Referral and follow-up

Turn a screening result into a documented, patient-centred continuity plan.

  1. Explain the recommended next step in the patient’s preferred language.
  2. Record the destination, planned date and contact route for follow-up.
  3. Use the clinical queue to review pending and overdue cases.
  4. Record actual outcomes; never mark a referral complete without confirmation.
Not started
Module 04 · 8 min

Privacy, consent and role access

Keep patient information available only to the people who need it for care.

  1. Lock or sign out of a shared device when leaving it unattended.
  2. Do not use screenshots, personal messaging apps or unapproved exports for patient data.
  3. Access only the records needed for your assigned work.
  4. Report a lost device, incorrect access or possible data exposure immediately.
Not started
